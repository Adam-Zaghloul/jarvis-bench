# Board 10000000000012 — status LED never blinks → C4 open

**Board / rev:** DEMO-1 rev A
**Date:** 2026-02-17
**Status:** RESOLVED

## Symptom
Power on, rails present, LED dark, no response to the button.

## Measurements
- 5V at C20: 5.02 V; 3V3 at C30: 3.31 V
- U3 pin 10 (XOUT): no 8 MHz clock
- Y1 swapped with a known-good crystal: still no clock
- C4 (power off): open, expected 22 pF

## Root cause
C4 open. Without its load capacitor, Y1 did not start.

## Fix
Replaced C4. 8 MHz at pin 10, LED blinks.
