# Normally low-impedance nets (power off, to GND)

Known-good readings from the demo board. A reading near these is not a fault.

| Net | Reading to GND [M] | Why it reads low |
|---|---|---|
| VIN | 2.4 kΩ | U1 input divider |
| 5V | 850 Ω | U2 input and U1 feedback divider |
| 3V3 | 1.6 kΩ | U3 supply pins |
